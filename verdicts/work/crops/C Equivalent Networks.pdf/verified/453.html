Mode norms in (II) $N_{n,v}^{(\text{II})} = \int_{-b}^{+b} \int_{-b}^{+b} \cos(\eta_n y) \cos(\eta_\nu z) \cdot \cos(\eta_{n'} y) \cos(\eta_{\nu'} z) \, dy \, dz$
$N_{n,v}^{(\text{II})} = b^2 N_n^{(y)} \cdot N_\nu^{(z)}$; $N_n^{(y)} = \begin{cases} 0; & n \neq n' \\ 1; & n = n' \neq 0 \\ 2; & n = n' = 0 \end{cases}$;
$N_\nu^{(z)} = \begin{cases} 0; & \nu \neq \nu' \\ 1; & \nu = \nu' \neq 0 \\ 2; & \nu = \nu' = 0 \end{cases}$
