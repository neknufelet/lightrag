Mode coupling (I)–(II)$S^{\mu,\nu}_{m,n}=\int_{-a}^{+a}\cos(\varepsilon_m y)\cos(\eta_n y)\,dy\int_{-a}^{+a}\cos(\varepsilon_\mu z)\cos(\chi_\nu z)\,dz$
$=S_{m,n}\cdot S^{\mu,\nu}$

$S_{m,n}=\begin{cases}a\left(\dfrac{\sin\left(a(\varepsilon_m-\eta_n)\right)}{a(\varepsilon_m-\eta_n)}+\dfrac{\sin\left(a(\varepsilon_m+\eta_n)\right)}{a(\varepsilon_m+\eta_n)}\right);&\varepsilon_m\neq\eta_n\\2a;&\varepsilon_m=\eta_n=0\\a\left(1+\dfrac{\sin\left(a(\varepsilon_m+\eta_n)\right)}{a(\varepsilon_m+\eta_n)}\right);&\varepsilon_m=\eta_n\neq0\end{cases}$

$S^{\mu,\nu}=\begin{cases}a\left(\dfrac{\sin\left(a(\varepsilon_\mu-\chi_\nu)\right)}{a(\varepsilon_\mu-\chi_\nu)}+\dfrac{\sin\left(a(\varepsilon_\mu+\chi_\nu)\right)}{a(\varepsilon_\mu+\chi_\nu)}\right);&\varepsilon_\mu\neq\chi_\nu\\2a;&\varepsilon_\mu=\chi_\nu=0\\a\left(1+\dfrac{\sin\left(a(\varepsilon_\mu+\chi_\nu)\right)}{a(\varepsilon_\mu+\chi_\nu)}\right);&\varepsilon_\mu=\chi_\nu\neq0\end{cases}$
